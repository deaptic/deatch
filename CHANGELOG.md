# Changelog

What's new in each version of Deatch, newest first.

## v0.4.4 — 26 November 2026

See what's new in each update, right in the app.

### New

- A What's new page lists every release. Open it from the sparkle button in the title bar.
- After an update, a popup shows its highlights, with a link to the full list.

### Improved

- Release pages on GitHub include these notes too.

## v0.4.3 — 26 November 2026

Right-click menus work properly again, and event icons in compact mode look tidier.

### Improved

- In compact mode, event icons are now small coloured badges. Hover one to see what kind of event it is.
- The widget preview in Settings > Widgets shows your own name, colour and profile picture.

### Fixed

- Copy, Pin, Delete and Raid in right-click menus work again.

## v0.4.2 — 25 November 2026

Show your chat on stream with the new OBS widgets.

### New

- Widgets show your chat on stream as an OBS Browser Source. Style each one, hide !commands or chosen users, and preview it live in Settings > Widgets.

### Fixed

- The message box empties as soon as you send, even if you keep typing.
- Profile pictures that are already loaded no longer fade in again.

## v0.4.1 — 25 November 2026

Link previews, a live outline on your own channel, and clearer chat events.

### New

- Links from trusted sites show a preview, and Deatch asks before opening links from other sites. Trusted sites are listed in Settings > Embeds.
- Your own channel in the channel list has a red outline while you're live, with the same hover card as other channels.

### Improved

- Chat events are clearer: who did it in the title, short details below, clickable names, and their own colour for moderation.
- Frequently used emotes count the emotes you send, not only the ones you pick from the emote picker.
- Busy chats run more smoothly, and the app looks more consistent in light and dark themes.
- The Link browser extension skips private tabs and handles replaced tabs.

### Fixed

- Unread counts ignore hidden messages and clear when you mark a channel as read.
- Your settings are kept if saving fails.
- Escape closes suggestion lists, and focus returns where it was after you close a menu.

## v0.4.0 — 24 November 2026

Timers, chat history search, and moderation from the keyboard.

### New

- Timers post repeating messages in your channel while you're live. Set them up in Settings > Timers.
- Ctrl+F searches your saved chat history and jumps to the message, in one channel or all of them.
- Keyboard moderation: select a message, press M, then one key to delete, time out or ban. Turn it on in Settings > Moderation.

### Improved

- Live status is easier to spot: a red, pulsing broadcast icon on the channel header, and offline channels dimmed in the channel list.
- Moderator tools can be switched off in Settings > Moderation to see the app as a viewer.
- Followed channels are off by default, with a one-click prompt in the channel list to show them.
